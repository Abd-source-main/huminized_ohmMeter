    Color code chart

    Hold the resistor so the tolerance band (the one set apart) is on the right. Read the digits left to right, then multiply.

    ColorDigitMultiplierTolerance

    4 bands

    digit, digit, multiplier, tolerance. Yellow Violet Red Gold = 47 × 100 = 4.7 kΩ ±5%.

    5 bands

    digit, digit, digit, multiplier, tolerance. Brown Black Black Red Brown = 100 × 100 = 10 kΩ ±1%.

    Gold and silver

    As a multiplier, gold is ×0.1 and silver is ×0.01. Yellow Violet Silver = 47 × 0.01 = 0.47 Ω = 470 mΩ.
